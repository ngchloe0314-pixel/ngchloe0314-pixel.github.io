        Which months warmed most in each city?

        Change in average monthly temperature anomaly between 1996–2005 and 2016–2025.

        Orange marks months that warmed. Blue marks months that cooled. Hover over a square to see both period averages. Cities are sorted by their average change across all 12 months.

        Source: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), Historical Weather API, ERA5 reanalysis: generated using Copernicus Climate Change Service information, ECMWF. Daily mean air temperature at 2 m, averaged by month; 40 cities, 1950 to 2025. Changes made: monthly means and differences from the 1961–1990 average computed for this class exercise.

        “Its own 1961–1990 average” means that city’s average temperature for the same calendar month in 1961–1990, so each city is compared only with itself. The whole-year figure is the average of the twelve monthly differences. ERA5 is a computer reconstruction of past weather on a grid of about 25 km, not a thermometer at the city centre, so it does not capture the extra warmth of dense city streets. July and January are the warm and cool months in the north; it is the other way round in the south. Rank 1 = warmest compared with its own average, among the 40 cities. Not endorsed by Open-Meteo, Copernicus or ECMWF.
