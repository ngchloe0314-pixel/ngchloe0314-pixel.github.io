01 / Coursework

Selected projects

People analytics

Exploring employee attrition

A group coursework project exploring when employees left and how engagement and job satisfaction varied.

Read the attrition case study

Question. When did recorded exits tend to happen?

Approach. I revisited the tenure-at-exit histogram from our group project. I used the recorded exit year to identify leavers in the Halden HR coursework dataset and grouped their tenure into one-year bands.

Finding. 539 of 988 recorded exits (54.6%) occurred before two years of tenure. This is the share of recorded exits, so it describes when leavers departed.

Next step. I’d compare two-year exit rates across hiring cohorts with enough follow-up, then look at whether onboarding experiences differed across those cohorts.
